# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep
discussion focused on the work. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through a private
[security advisory](https://github.com/delvera-ai/lithify/security/advisories/new) or by contacting a
maintainer directly. Maintainers will review reports promptly and confidentially and may remove
content or contributors that violate this code.
