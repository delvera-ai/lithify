**Changes**
- `tenacity/wait.py`: `wait_exponential.__call__` checks a new `_cap_reached(exponent)` before the power. The check only skips the power when the result is provably above `max` (int base ≥ 2, int multiplier ≥ 1, finite positive `max`, and `exponent > log2(max) − log2(multiplier) + 1`).
