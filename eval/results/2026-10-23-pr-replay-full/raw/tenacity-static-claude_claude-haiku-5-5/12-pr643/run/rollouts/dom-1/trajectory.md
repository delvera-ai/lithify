**Change**

- Tests: `TestEnabled::test_enabled_false_iterator_propagates_exception_unchanged` in `tests/test_tenacity.py` and the async twin in `tests/test_asyncio.py::TestAsyncEnabled`. Both assert the same exception object escapes and the body ran once.

- Reno note: `releasenotes/notes/enabled-false-iterator-5e2d9b1f7a3c4e86.yaml` under `fixes:`.

**Choices I made**
