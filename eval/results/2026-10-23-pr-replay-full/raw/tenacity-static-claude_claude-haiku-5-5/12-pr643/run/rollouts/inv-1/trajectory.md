final message:
`Retrying(enabled=False)` now runs the body once and re-raises the original exception on the iterator path (sync `for` and async `async for`). I also fixed direct calls to a `Retrying` object, since they had the same bug.

**Cause:** `enabled` was only checked in `wraps()`, which is the decorator path. The retry loop in `_begin_iter` (`tenacity/__init__.py`), shared by every entry point, never looked at it.

**Fix:** In `_begin_iter`, once an outcome exists and `enabled` is False, schedule `rs.outcome.result()` instead of a retry. That re-raises the original exception, and it reuses the existing give-up path. The change is four lines in one shared method, so `AsyncRetrying` and Tornado need no separate copy.

**Changes:**
- `tests/test_tenacity.py`: `test_enabled_false_iterator_runs_once` and `test_enabled_false_direct_call_runs_once`.
- `tests/test_asyncio.py`: `test_enabled_false_async_iterator_runs_once`.
- `releasenotes/notes/enabled-false-iterator-3f9c2a7d1e8b4c60.yaml`: a `fixes:` note.
