**What was wrong:** the iterator entry points never checked `enabled`, and `AttemptManager.__exit__` swallowed every exception. So the loop kept retrying until `RetryError`.

**Changes:**
- `tenacity/__init__.py`:

- `tenacity/asyncio/__init__.py`: `__anext__` raises `StopAsyncIteration` after the first attempt when disabled.
- Regression tests: `TestEnabled::test_enabled_false_iterator_runs_once` in `tests/test_tenacity.py` and `TestAsyncEnabled::test_enabled_false_async_iterator_runs_once` in `tests/test_asyncio.py`. Both failed on the unfixed code with `RetryError` after three attempts, and pass now.
