Bug: `retry_if_exception_message(message="")` raises `TypeError` ("missing 1 required argument 'message' or 'match'"), even though an empty string is a valid message to match (e.g. `str(RuntimeError()) == ""`). An empty `message` (or empty `match`) must be accepted and matched correctly; passing neither or both must still raise `TypeError`. The same applies to `retry_if_not_exception_message`.
