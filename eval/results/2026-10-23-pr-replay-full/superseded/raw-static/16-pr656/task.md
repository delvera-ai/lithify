Bug: `wait_exponential` can raise `ValueError: math domain error` instead of returning a wait time when `multiplier` and `max` are finite and positive but `max / multiplier` underflows to 0.0 (e.g. `wait_exponential(multiplier=1e300, max=1e-100)`), or when `multiplier` is not finite. It should return the capped wait instead; results for ordinary values must not change.
