BigQuery dialect: in BigQuery a bare `WEEK` date part means `WEEK(SUNDAY)` (weeks start on Sunday). When BigQuery truncation functions such as `DATE_TRUNC(x, WEEK)` (and the related timestamp/datetime/time truncations and `LAST_DAY`) are transpiled to other dialects, a bare `WEEK` must keep Sunday-start semantics, i.e. transpile exactly like the explicit `WEEK(SUNDAY)`. This matters for targets such as DuckDB, whose weeks start on Monday.
