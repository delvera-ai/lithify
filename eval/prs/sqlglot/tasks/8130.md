Trino dialect, inline SQL UDFs (the last part of the routine-body support, after `IF`, `CASE` and `WHILE`): parse and generate the remaining loop-control statements inside routine bodies:

```sql
[label:] LOOP ...; END LOOP
[label:] REPEAT ...; UNTIL cond END REPEAT
ITERATE label
LEAVE label
```

with optional labels on `LOOP` and `REPEAT`. They must round-trip in Trino.
