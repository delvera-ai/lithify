Presto/Trino → other dialects: `FROM_ISO8601_TIMESTAMP` and `FROM_ISO8601_DATE` now transpile to casts in other dialects, but `FROM_ISO8601_TIMESTAMP_NANOS` is still parsed as an unknown function and emitted verbatim (invalid in DuckDB, Snowflake, ...). Handle it like `FROM_ISO8601_TIMESTAMP`: round-trip in Presto/Trino and cast to a timestamp with time zone elsewhere.
