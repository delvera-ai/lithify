Optimizer: transpiling Spark's `LATERAL VIEW POSEXPLODE` to DuckDB produces SQL that runs in DuckDB, but `qualify()` cannot resolve a column in that generated SQL:

```sql
SELECT pos, val
FROM t
CROSS JOIN LATERAL (SELECT pos - 1 AS pos, val FROM UNNEST(t.arr) WITH ORDINALITY AS _t0(val, pos))
-- qualify(..., dialect="duckdb") raises OptimizeError
```

Columns produced by a `LATERAL` subquery must resolve in the outer query, including when the inner projection both reads and redefines a name.
