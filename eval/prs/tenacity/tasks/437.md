Feature: `AsyncRetrying` (and `@retry` on coroutine functions) should support `async` callbacks: when `before`, `after`, `before_sleep`, `retry_error_callback`, `retry`, `stop` or `wait` are coroutine functions, they must be awaited. Currently an async `retry_error_callback` produces "RuntimeWarning: coroutine was never awaited". Synchronous callbacks must keep working unchanged.
