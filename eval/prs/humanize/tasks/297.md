Bug: `humanize.naturalday()` and `humanize.naturaldate()` give the wrong answer for timezone-aware datetimes. They compare against the system's local date, so when the date in the datetime's own timezone differs from the system date the result is off by a day. Example: if it is currently `2023-10-15 23:00 UTC`, a datetime 7 hours later expressed in AEDT (`+11:00`) is still "today" in AEDT, but humanize reports "tomorrow". For tz-aware datetimes, "today" must be determined in that datetime's timezone; behaviour for naive datetimes and plain dates must not change.
