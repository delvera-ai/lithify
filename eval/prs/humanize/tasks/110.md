Feature: add `humanize.natural_list(items)` that converts a list of items into a natural-language list: items are converted with `str()`, joined with commas, and the last two joined with "and". For example `natural_list(["one", "two", "three"])` returns `"one, two and three"`, `natural_list(["one", "two"])` returns `"one and two"`, and a single item is returned on its own. Export it from the `humanize` package.
