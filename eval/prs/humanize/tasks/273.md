Bug: `humanize.intword()` chooses singular or plural based on the number before rounding, not on the rounded value it displays. With `humanize.i18n.activate("de_DE")`, `intword(1000000)` returns "1.0 Million" but `intword(1000001)` returns "1.0 Millionen". The plural form must be chosen from the value that is actually displayed.
